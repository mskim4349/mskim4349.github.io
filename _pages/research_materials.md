<a class="rf-back" href="/research/">&larr; Research Field</a>

<p class="rf-lead">Experimental evaluation of metals, polymers, and insulation foams under cryogenic and hydrogen conditions for liquid-hydrogen and cryogenic service.</p>

<div class="rf-sub">
  <h2>Cryogenic Tensile and Fracture Toughness of Metals</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Tensile behaviour and J-resistance fracture toughness of 304L and 316L stainless steels down to 20 K.</li>
    <li>Objective, operator-independent property determination (ASTM E3076 and E1820 compliance).</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="rf-fig2"><img src="/assets/img/research/detail/cryo_fracture_toughness.svg" alt="Cryogenic fracture toughness determination"></div>
</div>

<div class="rf-sub">
  <h2>Hydrogen Charging and Embrittlement</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Electrochemical and gaseous hydrogen charging, followed by tensile, impact, and fracture-toughness testing.</li>
    <li>Relates the change from dimple-dominated to quasi-cleavage fracture to the absorbed hydrogen.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="rf-fig2"><img src="/assets/img/research/detail/h_fracture_toughness.svg" alt="Fracture toughness of hydrogen-charged steel"></div>
</div>

<div class="rf-sub">
  <h2>Reinforced Polyurethane Foam Insulation</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Mechanical performance degradation of cryogenic insulation foam under repetitive low-energy impact.</li>
  </ul>

</div>
